How does it work?

FFT estimates the frequency distribution. Positive changes in spectral energy form an onset envelope. Autocorrelation over recent history estimates periodicity in the 80–180 BPM range. The 16-cell timeline shows detected onset activity within estimated beat intervals. Energy shifts are heuristic, not reliable drop predictions. Microphone clipping, automatic gain, crowds and echoes can affect all results. Level is relative digital signal amplitude (dBFS). The dedicated meter computes a roughly one-second RMS level, a session power average and a maximum one-second level. Optional reference calibration estimates unweighted SPL; it does not provide dBA or a reliable hearing exposure measurement.

Use Safari on an HTTPS website, allow microphone permission and keep the screen awake. The app never records or sends audio anywhere. Demo is synthetic and silent.
